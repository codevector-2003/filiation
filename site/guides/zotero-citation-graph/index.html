Turn your Zotero library into a citation graph

Zotero is very good at keeping references and formatting citations. Filiation adds what it does not do: a map of what your papers cite, the papers you are missing, and search inside every PDF.

Keep Zotero for what it does well

Zotero stores your references, syncs them, and puts citations and a bibliography into your writing. Filiation does none of that, on purpose. It reads your Zotero library, read-only, and never changes it, so nothing about the way you work in Zotero has to change.

What Filiation adds

  • The citations between your papers. Each paper's reference list comes from OpenAlex, already matched to the works it names, so you see which of your papers cite which, drawn as a map.
  • The papers you don't have yet. Filiation follows your papers' references outward, most-cited first, and shows the works your library keeps citing that are not in it.
  • Their PDFs, when they are free to read. It downloads the legal open-access copies, never from behind a paywall, and copies the PDFs you already keep in Zotero.
  • Search inside every PDF, to the page. A few keywords find the passage and its page, lit on the PDF.
  • Your AI assistant, on your own papers. Over MCP, Claude, Cursor, GitHub Copilot or Gemini can search the library and quote it with pages.

How to bring Zotero in

  1. Let Zotero talk to other apps

    In Zotero 7, choose Edit › Settings › Advanced and tick Allow other applications on this computer to communicate with Zotero.

  2. Bring the library in

    In the Filiation app, open the Inbox and choose Zotero. From a terminal, see what would come in first, then bring it in:

    fil zotero --dry-run fil zotero

  3. Grow the map

    Follow the references outward and keep the open-access PDFs:

    fil expand fil fetch

No Zotero on this computer? Export the library as Zotero RDF with its files and use fil zotero --from "My Library.rdf". Every option is in the Zotero documentation.

Questions

Does Filiation change my Zotero library?

No. It reads through Zotero's local API and writes nothing back. Your Zotero library stays exactly as it was.

Does it replace Zotero?

No. Filiation does not format citations or build bibliographies. Keep Zotero for writing; use Filiation to see and search what your papers rest on.

Are my Zotero tags kept?

If you ask: fil zotero --tags turns the tags you added into labels.

Why do some papers have no reference list?

OpenAlex has reference lists for most papers in the sciences and far fewer in the humanities. Filiation shows this for every paper, so a thin map reads as the data, not as a fault.
